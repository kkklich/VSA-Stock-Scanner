# Shortlist: volume-based, medium-term, long-only methods

A head-start catalogue of established methods that already match all three
criteria (volume-central · medium-term hold · bullish/long-only). **Not the
final answer** — confirm each against live sources and find real dated examples
before presenting it, and add methods beyond this list if the user wants. Do the
verification by *reading* sources and real data via web tools, never by writing
code.

The methods with the most independently verifiable track records are marked ⭐.

---

## 1. ⭐ Pocket Pivot / Volume Dry-Up (VDU)
- **Source:** Gil Morales & Chris Kacher — *Trade Like an O'Neil Disciple*
  (2010), *In the Trading Cockpit with the O'Neil Disciples* (2012);
  virtueofselfishinvesting.com. (Both are documented former O'Neil-fund
  managers.)
- **Setup:** inside a constructive base, buy a day whose up-volume exceeds the
  highest *down*-volume of the prior ~10 days ("pocket pivot"), often right after
  volume has dried up (VDU). Volume *is* the trigger.
- **Fits:** volume = the signal itself · hold weeks–months · long-only.

## 2. ⭐ CANSLIM base breakout on volume
- **Source:** William O'Neil — *How to Make Money in Stocks*; Investor's Business
  Daily. (Decades-long published methodology.)
- **Setup:** breakout above a proper base (cup-with-handle, flat base) with
  volume ≥ ~40–50% above the 50-day average. Weak-volume breakouts are ignored.
- **Fits:** volume confirms the breakout · medium-term swing/position · bullish.

## 3. ⭐ VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern)
- **Source:** Mark Minervini — *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard* (2013). (US
  Investing Championship winner — a real, verifiable competition record.)
- **Setup:** a base of successive tighter pullbacks where **volume contracts**
  into the pivot, then **expands** on the breakout. The volume dry-up →
  expansion is integral.
- **Fits:** volume contraction/expansion in the rule · medium-term · bullish.

## 4. Stan Weinstein Stage-2 breakout
- **Source:** Stan Weinstein — *Secrets for Profiting in Bull and Bear Markets*
  (1988).
- **Setup:** price crosses above a flattening/rising **30-week moving average**
  on a clear **surge in volume** — the transition from basing (Stage 1) to
  advancing (Stage 2).
- **Fits:** volume confirms the stage change · medium-term (Stage 2 runs months)
  · bullish (only the long side is used here).

## 5. VSA accumulation signals — Spring, Test, Sign of Strength, No Supply
- **Source:** Tom Williams — *Master the Markets*; the TradeGuider/VSA tradition
  (built on Richard Wyckoff). This is the engine the app already runs.
- **Setup:** bullish VSA events where low supply / strong demand shows up in the
  volume–spread–close relationship (Spring, successful Test, SOS, No Supply).
- **Fits:** volume/effort is the whole premise · medium-term · use only the
  bullish signals (skip Upthrust, SOW, No Demand — those are the bearish side).

## 6. Wyckoff accumulation → mark-up
- **Source:** Richard Wyckoff — *The Richard D. Wyckoff Method of Trading and
  Investing in Stocks*; the Wyckoff schematic.
- **Setup:** the accumulation phases (Selling Climax, Automatic Rally, Secondary
  Test, Spring, Sign of Strength, Last Point of Support) read through volume,
  entering as mark-up begins.
- **Fits:** volume defines each phase · medium-term · bullish (accumulation).

## 7. Darvas Box breakout
- **Source:** Nicolas Darvas — *How I Made $2,000,000 in the Stock Market*
  (1960). (Famous but largely anecdotal record — note that when citing.)
- **Setup:** buy a breakout above the top of a "box" (a defined consolidation
  range) **confirmed by a jump in volume**; trail the box upward.
- **Fits:** volume confirms the breakout · medium-term · bullish.

## 8. On-Balance Volume (OBV) / Accumulation-Distribution confirmation
- **Source:** Joseph Granville — *Granville's New Key to Stock Market Profits*
  (1963) for OBV; Marc Chaikin for the Accumulation/Distribution line & Chaikin
  Money Flow.
- **Setup:** a rising OBV / A-D line (or CMF > 0) confirming or leading a price
  uptrend signals accumulation; enter long on confirmation.
- **Fits:** pure volume-flow indicator · medium-term trend · bullish use.

## 9. Relative Volume (RVOL) breakout
- **Source:** general scanner methodology (Finviz "Unusual Volume", O'Neil's
  volume rules). The app already ships this as `/volume-surge`.
- **Setup:** unusually high recent volume vs the stock's own baseline
  *together with* an up move / breakout — the volume marks institutional
  participation.
- **Fits:** volume ratio is the screen · medium-term when paired with a base
  breakout · bullish when filtered to up moves.

1. Pull the **exact rules** and the **track-record evidence** from the source
   (WebSearch/WebFetch).
2. Find **2–3 real, dated examples** — from the source's own case studies, or by
   reading real price/volume history (stooq.pl / Yahoo), GPW preferred.
3. Fill the SKILL.md output template, always with sources and exact dates, and
   label each example as *documented* vs *independently verified*.
